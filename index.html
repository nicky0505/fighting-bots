THE BASICS

What Are Hydro Fighting Bots?

Hydraulic-powered robots that use fluid pressure to create real, controlled movement — just like the machines used in industry and medicine.

👤 Who?

Engineers, doctors, factory workers, and athletes in training.

🤖 What?

Robots powered by fluid pressure to create controlled movement.

🏭 Where?

Machinery, medical devices, and industrial robots.

⏱️ When?

Any task needing strength, precision, and repeatability.

💡 Why?

To make dangerous or repetitive tasks safer and more efficient.

⚙️ How?

A small force pushes fluid through a tube, transferring that force into movement.
